About Strandex

Strandex provides wire rope and lifting gear inspection for the construction industry in Singapore. We assess the ropes, slings, and rigging that carry live loads on cranes and hoists — work where a missed defect has real consequences.

Every inspection follows the care, maintenance, and discard criteria set out in ISO 4309, the international standard for crane wire ropes. We check for broken wires, corrosion, distortion, and diameter loss, and report findings in plain terms: what we found, what it means, and what to do next.

Workplace safety governs how we work, on-site and off it. Strandex [holds / is pursuing] bizSAFE Level 3 certification — an audited Risk Management Plan aligned with Singapore's WSH regulations, the same standard most main contractors require of the vendors they engage.
